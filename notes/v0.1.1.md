## What's new

- The newest release of a port is now the highest version tag. Before, GitHub's "latest" could point at a toolchain build such as `sysroot-2026.11.09.2`, which a port like Midtown Madness 3 or Pinyon Shift publishes next to its game releases; the panel then showed that as the port's version.

## Putting it on the handheld

If 0.1.0 is installed, open Armada Ports, press **Check for updates** and then **Update Armada Ports to 0.1.1** at the bottom of the panel; Decky asks to confirm and reloads it. A fresh install works as for 0.1.0: `bash install.sh` next to `ArmadaPorts-v0.1.1.zip` in Desktop Mode.
