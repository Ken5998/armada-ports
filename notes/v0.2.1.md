## What's new

- **Midtown Madness 3**: a build that stopped half way (a reboot, a crash, Stop) now shows as unfinished, with **Finish the build** to pick it up from the game files already copied. Before, the old game binary made the panel take it for a finished install, with no way to resume from the plugin.
- While a build runs, the plugin asks the system not to sleep or shut down, so a build of an hour is not cut off by the screen going to sleep. A reboot started by Steam's own update may still go ahead: turn off automatic restarts for Steam updates while you build.

## Putting it on the handheld

From 0.2.0: open Armada Ports, press **Check for updates**, then **Update Armada Ports to 0.2.1** at the bottom of the panel. A fresh install: `bash install.sh` next to `ArmadaPorts-v0.2.1.zip` in Desktop Mode.
