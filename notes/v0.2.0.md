## What's new

- **Midtown Madness 3**, **Saints Reborn** and **Rusty MCLA** now install, update and go into Steam from the panel, like Pinyon Shift and FIFA Street. Installs made with their launchers are found as they are, so the panel shows which version you have and offers the update when a newer one is out (Midtown Madness 3 0.1.x shows the update to 0.2.0, the Vulkan rework).
- Saints Reborn is followed like FIFA Street: new scripts on its main branch count as an update too.
- Rusty MCLA keeps the resolution, frame cap, render scale and fill settings you chose in its launcher when the plugin updates it.
- Road Trip Adventure, ReSkate and Azimuth Turnip are still listed with their newest release only.

## Putting it on the handheld

From 0.1.x: open Armada Ports, press **Check for updates**, then **Update Armada Ports to 0.2.0** at the bottom of the panel. A fresh install: `bash install.sh` next to `ArmadaPorts-v0.2.0.zip` in Desktop Mode.
