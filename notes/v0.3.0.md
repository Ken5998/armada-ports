## What's new

- **Armada Ports is now an app.** It sits in your Steam library like a game and is made for the controller: the ports as a row of covers with their SteamGridDB artwork, a page for each one with what it is, how it runs on the RP6, what it needs, how long the build takes and what the newest release changed, and the buttons to install, update, play, add to Steam or uninstall it.
- **Play from the app.** The game starts in the app's place and you come back to its page when you quit. A game that is also in your library keeps the Turnip driver and performance profile you gave it in Armada Control.
- **Disc images found for you.** Install lists the images in your home folder, Downloads, Games and on the SD card; a folder browser covers the rest. Everything works with the touchscreen too.
- **Two new ports to install:** FH2 Recompiled and Skate 3 Recomp.
- **The Quick Access panel is now small.** It opens the app, shows running builds and new releases, and updates Armada Ports. The plugin's real job is now being the app's link to Steam: it adds the app and the games to your library at once, with artwork, without restarting Steam.

## Getting it on the RP6

On 0.2.x: Quick Access menu, Decky, Armada Ports, **Check for updates**, then **Update Armada Ports to 0.3.0**. A few seconds after Decky reloads it, **Armada Ports** is in your library with its own artwork.

First time: in Desktop Mode put `ArmadaPorts-v0.3.0.zip` and `install.sh` in one folder and run `bash install.sh` there.

The download is bigger than before (about 25 MB) because the app is inside it. Ports you installed with the plugin or with their launchers show up in the app as installed, nothing to redo.
